About JinoSoft

How we design our apps

JinoSoft focuses on how people save information and find it again. Account credentials and daily records serve different purposes, so each app is designed around the information it handles.

01

On-device storage

LockSheet works without a server account and encrypts the account details stored on your device.

02

Records by date

Beecabi organizes your pet's daily care, vet visits, and expenses by date, with search to help you find them.

03

Backup and export

Both apps support backup and export for moving devices or keeping a separate copy of your data.
