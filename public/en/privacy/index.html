Effective date: November 3, 2026

Jinosoft (the “Operator”) operates jinosoft.com. This policy applies to this website and its app information pages. Privacy practices within each mobile app are described separately by that app.

This website does not provide user accounts, comments, payments, or a contact form. If you contact us by email, we may process your email address and message to respond and provide follow-up support. We delete the correspondence without undue delay once the inquiry and necessary follow-up support are complete, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. The hosting provider processes this information under its own practices. See About GitHub Pages and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and analytics

We currently do not install advertising, visitor analytics, or tracking cookies on this website. Technical information processed by GitHub Pages or other service providers to deliver and secure the site is governed by their policies. If you follow a link to Google Play or our tech blog, the destination site’s privacy policy applies. See the tech blog privacy policy.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell visitor information or independently share it with third parties. GitHub may process access information as part of website hosting. Email is transmitted through the mail service used by the sender and recipient.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy or our handling of information, contact support@jinosoft.com.

6. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if website features or information-handling practices change.

If advertising is added to this website, we will update this policy to describe relevant processing by advertising providers, including the use of cookies, web beacons, and IP addresses. No ads are currently displayed here.
